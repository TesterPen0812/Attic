Attic render lab — 5 November 2026

Answer: I don't know what causes the idle first-click delay. Turning off all 24 tested render-cost sites together did not cure it. Across four alternating 30-second-idle page-switch pairs, first changed window pixels were 133.5 ms [126.1–143.8] normally and 163.7 ms [160.2–164.7] with everything off; immediate repeats were 137.1 [127.6–144.7] and 167.9 [164.0–172.5] ms. The paired differences were −29.3 [−37.5 to −19.4] ms cold and −32.1 [−42.3 to −19.3] ms repeat: the disabled condition was slower. A separate earlier combined run was inconclusive (cold paired improvement +13.7 [−21.6 to 170.1] ms). This rules out those switches as a sufficient fix for the tested action, but does not identify the cause of the original intermittent 200–540 ms main-thread marker or its longer tail.

Method: One ad-hoc-signed, optimized Local-only Attic Render Lab on the Mac Air, with its own in-memory seeded data and bundle identity. Each site was tested in alternating normal/disabled launches after 30 seconds idle, with an immediate repeat (normally four valid pairs; invalid captures excluded); load was recorded before every trial. Real AX-targeted pointer clicks and wheel events drove Tasks, Notes, Calendar, Settings, Search and +. A ScreenCaptureKit stream of the targeted lab window recorded the first material change across a 64×64 RGB sample, timed from mouse release for clicks and the first wheel event for scrolling; it cannot see physical panel scanout or pixels in another window. App frame, page and motion tracing were enabled. All switches default to off and are restricted to this lab identity.

1. Blind spot — Confirmed. Window-pixel change is later than input even when a display-link callback occurs; frame arrival minus the capture's display timestamp was typically 1.4–3.2 ms, with one noisy trial up to 11 ms. A 60 Hz display adds frame sampling granularity and the pixel threshold can miss subtler early changes. The minimal SwiftUI control on the same Air had cold median 11.6 ms (9.1–106.3, five trials) and repeat median about 12.4 ms. A universal macOS first-click penalty is unlikely, although one control trial stalled. This measurement does not apportion remaining delay between app layout/commit and render server.

2. Masks — Present in Calendar scroll, tag bar, island, panel clipping, menu, dropdown and Settings. Calendar scroll-mask removal changed cold first pixels from 60.4 [52.1–67.3] to 66.6 [54.0–67.3] ms and repeats 48.9 to 50.6 ms; paired ranges cross zero. Tasks scroll without panel clipping: cold 49.5 [47.6–51.8] to 69.6 [46.5–93.7] ms, repeat 35.1 to 25.8 ms (paired improvement +5.8 [0.5–15.6]), not a cold cure. Tag/island masks and panel clipping during tab/page switches were inconsistent within spread. Dropdown insertion/content masks did not show replicated benefit: content-mask cold paired savings +15.2 [−0.2 to 30.1] ms forward versus −7.2 [−15.2 to 12.2] ms reversed. Dropdown viewport clipping gave small cold savings +18.2 [3.9–19.7] ms in three pairs and +3.1 [2.2–12.9] ms in four reversed pairs; repeat results crossed zero. Changing its clipping could alter overflow, and the cold effect is close to a display frame. Detached menu and Settings scroll changes were not measurable with the selected pixel target.

3. Shadows — Present on panel, controls, island and Settings preview. Panel-shadow removal: cold 119.5 to 129.2 ms; repeat 120.6 to 109.3 ms, with paired ranges crossing zero. Island-shadow removal initially improved cold by +16.1 [5.9–27.5] ms, but reversed-order replication yielded −10.4 [−40.2 to 2.8] ms. Settings preview shadow initially appeared to save +32.1 [24.5–32.9] ms cold and +30.2 [3.3–31.2] ms repeat (three pairs); reversed order showed −0.5 [−16.0 to 1.8] cold and −1.3 [−17.4 to 18.2] repeat (four pairs). Neither shadow gain reproduced; Search/control shadow trials also showed no consistent benefit.

4. Delays after action — Top-row fade has a 90 ms scheduled delay; removing it changed cold first pixels 158.2 to 163.7 ms and repeats 133.4 to 128.7 ms, with paired ranges crossing zero: no measured first-pixel saving. Bottom-row return has a 90 ms delayed animation: removing it improved repeat bottom-strip settling by +65.3 [11.5–85.5] ms (328.9 to 262.9), although repeat first pixels got slower (98.7 to 132.5 ms). Island label waits 400 ms on initial hover and settles 300 ms after pointer leaves; those hover/exit schedules were not measured as pixel delays. Dropdown motion lasts 180 ms opening/120 ms closing with 30 ms content staging; disabling reveal gave cold paired change −0.9 [−18.1 to 17.5] ms and repeat +9.3 [−0.8 to 19.2] ms: no reliable first-pixel saving.

5. Per-frame layout — Present in tag-bar width and Notes layout selection. Disabling tag-width animation saved +20.8 [17.8–21.9] ms on repeats (130.4 to 109.0); reversed-order replication saved +16.6 [4.8–29.4] ms (113.2 to 96.3). Cold improvement did not reproduce. Disabling Notes selection motion improved cold first pixels +21.4 [16.5–37.3] ms (202.2 [181.9–265.2] to 180.9 [165.4–227.8]) and cold bottom-strip settling +237.5 [233.3–249.7] ms, but repeat first pixels worsened (156.5 to 180.7). Composer growth did not reliably help; removing whole-page motion worsened repeat page changes by 16.3 [4.8–29.2] ms. Panel travel already has a fixed hosting layer, with no measured case for replacing its animation with an additive Core Animation spring.

Action coverage: Tasks/Notes/Calendar Search normal cold medians were 161.8/100.1/112.2 ms; + were 266.6/201.2/200.8 ms, with wide Tasks + spread [187.9–498.3]. Repeat medians were Search 143.1/94.4/94.7 and + 182.7/170.2/160.8 ms. Tested control-shadow and composer-clip toggles had no dependable first-click saving on these paths. Island page changes, Tasks tab changes, Calendar and Tasks scrolling were sampled. Tasks row cold click with panel clipping disabled: 163.0 [157.1–260.9] ms normally versus 157.2 [146.8–166.8] ms disabled, paired gain +7.5 [−9.7 to 110.8] ms (four pairs), inconclusive.

For the owner: The smallest appearance-preserving candidates are to stop rerunning the tag-bar width layout every animation frame by giving its animated face a fixed layer, and to animate Notes layout changes with a fixed transform/snapshot while committing layout once. Neither shadow removal nor wholesale removal of masks is justified by replicated first-pixel results. Removing Notes layout animation or the 90 ms bottom return delay outright changes the intended feel; those are opt-in design choices. None addresses the original first-click mystery by itself.

Limits and checks: Four pairs per condition are a noise check, not a statistical performance guarantee; interleaved trials had changing Air load, and first-pixel timing differs from fully settled appearance. The earlier main-thread IdleLatency ticks, including an idle median 364 ms from press and p90 1045 ms, measure a different stage and workload. Detached menu pixels and Settings scroll could not be isolated; task-row repeats were intentionally omitted because selecting a row may dismiss the panel. Project generation/verification and optimized Mac Air build passed. A scoped headless unit-test attempt exited 70 after an Xcode launcher assertion before test results; this is not a test pass. This branch is an isolated experiment and is not for merge.
